MODA

Privacy Policy · Effective 13 October 2026

The short version

Moda keeps your wardrobe on your phone. There is no account, no Moda server, and no copy of your clothes held by us. We cannot see your photos, your outfits or what you wear, because they never reach us.

Three things to be precise about. If you switch weather suggestions on, an approximate location goes to Apple's weather service. If you back your phone up to iCloud, your Moda data is part of that backup — held by Apple under your own iCloud settings, not by us. And if you email us, we hold that email; there is a section below on exactly what happens to it.

What Moda stores, and where

Everything listed here is written to your device's own storage and stays there:

  • Garment photographs you take or import, saved as image files in Moda's private app folder
  • Garment details — names, categories, colours, brands, prices and notes you enter
  • Outfits you build or save, including planned outfits on the calendar
  • Wear history — which outfits you logged and on which dates — and the streaks and badges derived from it
  • Laundry state — which items you have marked as needing washing
  • Your Style DNA answers, if you take the quiz
  • Settings such as your appearance choice, notification times and whether weather suggestions are on

None of this is transmitted to us. We operate no servers that receive your wardrobe data, so we have no ability to read, recover, sell or disclose it. The one qualification is device backups, described next.

Device and iCloud backups

Moda does not exclude its data from your device's backups. If you back your iPhone up to iCloud or to a computer, your garment photographs and wardrobe data are included in that backup, exactly as with most other apps. An iCloud backup is stored by Apple on your behalf, encrypted, under your Apple Account and Apple's terms — it is not accessible to us, and we are not told it exists.

We mention this because we would rather be exact than make a cleaner-sounding claim. “Moda keeps no copy of your wardrobe” is true. “Your data never leaves the device under any circumstances” would not be, if you use iCloud Backup. You control this: iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › iCloud Backup, where Moda can be switched off individually.

The practical upside is that if your phone is lost or replaced, restoring from a backup usually brings your wardrobe back — something we could never do for you ourselves.

The home screen widget

If you add Moda's widget, the app writes a small summary — today's suggested outfit, your streak count and a few thumbnail images — into a shared container on your device so the widget can display it. This container is private to Moda and its widget. It does not leave your device.

Permissions Moda asks for

Camera and photo library

Used only so you can photograph garments or choose existing pictures to add to your closet. Images you select are copied into Moda's own storage on your device. Moda does not scan, index or upload your photo library, and does not read photos you have not chosen.

Location

Off unless you turn it on. If you enable weather-aware suggestions in Settings and grant location access, Moda obtains an approximate location (accurate to roughly a kilometre, not your exact position) and passes it to Apple's WeatherKit service to retrieve the local forecast. Apple's handling of that request is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Moda does not store your location history, and does not send your location anywhere else. Results are cached for about an hour to limit how often this happens. You can switch it off at any time in Settings, or deny the permission entirely — everything else in Moda works exactly the same without it.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled and delivered locally by your device. No push server is involved, and we are not notified when you receive or open one.

What Moda does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-ups, logins or profiles
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising, ad identifiers or advertising SDKs
  • No cookies, pixels or similar tracking technologies
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information — we hold none to share
  • No collection of your name, email address, phone number or payment details
  • No in-app purchases in this version, and so no billing information of any kind
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency

Third parties

Moda contains no third-party code. It uses no analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting or data-broker services, and it bundles no software development kits from other companies — it is built entirely on Apple's own frameworks.

We have gone through the app's source and the frameworks it links against to establish this. The outside parties we have identified are two, and both are companies you are already dealing with by owning an iPhone:

  • Apple. WeatherKit receives an approximate location if you opt in to weather suggestions. Apple also runs the App Store you downloaded Moda from, and holds your iCloud backup if you use one. All of that is governed by Apple's own policies and none of it is visible to us, beyond the anonymous, aggregated download figures Apple reports to every developer.
  • GitHub. Hosts the privacy and terms pages you are reading, and nothing else. It has no involvement with the app.

There is no cloud database, no image-processing service, no background-removal service, no analytics provider, no attribution or marketing platform, and no sign-in provider — because Moda has no account to sign in to. Your garment photographs are not sent to any company for processing, ours or anyone else's: the colour detection runs on your phone.

Moda contains no networking code of its own at all. The only component that can reach the internet is Apple's own WeatherKit framework, and only when you have switched weather suggestions on.

This website

The page you are reading is a plain static page. It runs no scripts, sets no cookies, loads no fonts or resources from anywhere else, and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind. Nothing here reports your visit back to us. (If you choose to email us, we of course receive whatever you send — that is you contacting us, not the page tracking you.)

It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers may log technical request data such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not have access to those logs and we do not collect anything from this site ourselves.

When you contact us

Everything above describes the app. Writing to us is different, and we should be straight about it.

If you email modaapp.co@gmail.com — to ask something, report a bug, raise a privacy question or send feedback — we receive your email address, your name if you sign off with it, and whatever you choose to put in the message, including any screenshots or files you attach. That is personal information, and for as long as we hold it we are responsible for it.

We use it only to answer you and to fix what you have reported. We do not add you to a mailing list, we do not use it for marketing, and we do not pass it to anyone. The address is a Gmail account, so the message sits on Google's servers under Google's terms, as any email does.

We keep correspondence while it is useful — typically until the question is resolved and a reasonable period after, in case you write back — and delete it after that. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time and we will.

Please do not send us things we have not asked for and do not need. If you are reporting a bug, a description is usually enough; we do not need your wardrobe export unless we specifically ask, and we would rather not hold it.

Security reports. If you believe you have found a security problem in Moda, please write to the same address with "security" in the subject line. We would much rather hear from you than not, we will not take issue with anyone who reports something in good faith, and we will tell you what we have done about it.

How long your information is kept

For as long as you choose to keep it. Because the data lives on your device, we neither retain nor delete it — you do. There is no copy held elsewhere that could outlive your device.

Your choices and your rights

Because nothing leaves your phone, you exercise most of your rights directly inside the app rather than by asking us:

  • See your information: it is all visible in the app
  • Correct it: edit or delete any garment, outfit or logged wear at any time
  • Export it: Settings › Export Wardrobe produces a file containing your garments, outfits and wear history. Once you save or send that file it is yours to look after — it sits wherever you put it, under that app or service's protections rather than Moda's, and we have no control over it
  • Delete it: Settings › Reset All Data erases your garments and their photograph files, outfits, wear history, badges, Style DNA and the copy the widget reads. Deleting the app removes everything it holds. Note that an existing device or iCloud backup may still contain a copy until that backup is replaced or deleted — that part is managed in your iOS settings, not by us
  • Withdraw consent: turn weather suggestions off, or revoke camera, photo, location or notification permissions in iOS Settings

Should you believe we hold personal information about you, write to us and we will respond within 30 days, usually much sooner — though in practice we expect the answer to be that we hold none. If you are in a jurisdiction with comparable law, such as the UK or the EU, equivalent rights apply and the mechanisms above satisfy them.

Security

Your Moda data is protected by your device's own security: your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and iOS's protection of each app's private storage from other apps. We recommend keeping a device passcode enabled and your iOS version current.

Because Moda stores everything locally and we keep no copy, we cannot recover your wardrobe if your device is lost, damaged, reset, or if you delete the app. A device or iCloud backup is your safeguard here, not us. Exporting your wardrobe from Settings now and then is a sensible second one.

Children

Moda is not directed at young children, and is intended for users aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please read this policy together with a parent or guardian before using the app.

Moda does not ask your age, and it has no mechanism for obtaining or verifying parental consent. It also does not collect personal information from you or send any to us: your photographs and wardrobe stay on your own device. POPIA places particular requirements on the processing of a child's personal information, and we have written this policy to describe accurately what the app does so that a parent or guardian can judge it for themselves.

A parent or guardian who does not want a child using Moda can delete it from the device or restrict installation through iOS Screen Time. If you are a parent and have a question, or believe a child's information has somehow reached us, please contact us and we will respond promptly.
