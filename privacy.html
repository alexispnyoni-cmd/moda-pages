MODA

Privacy Policy · Effective June 17, 2026

Moda is a personal wardrobe and styling app designed with your privacy in mind. We believe your style is personal, and your data should stay that way.

Data That Stays on Your Device

All of your wardrobe data — garment photos, outfits, wear history, preferences, and streaks — is stored locally on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your data on any external server.

Camera & Photo Library

Moda requests access to your camera and photo library solely to let you photograph and import garment images into your wardrobe. These photos are saved to your device’s local storage and are never uploaded or shared.

Location

Moda may request your approximate location to provide weather-aware outfit suggestions using Apple’s WeatherKit. Your location data is sent directly to Apple’s weather service and is not collected, stored, or shared by Moda.

Subscriptions

Moda Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles on our servers
  • We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you can delete all of your Moda data at any time through Settings > Reset All Data, or by deleting the app from your device.

Children’s Privacy

Moda does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at modaapp.co@gmail.com.
