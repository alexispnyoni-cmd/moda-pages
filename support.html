Support

We're here to help. If you're experiencing an issue with Moda or have a question, check the common topics below or get in touch.

Manage Your Subscription

Moda Premium subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. To change or cancel your subscription, go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device.

Delete Your Data

All Moda data is stored locally on your device. To delete everything, open Moda and go to Settings > Reset All Data, or simply delete the app.

App Not Working as Expected?

Try closing and reopening the app, or restarting your device. If the issue persists, contact us with a description of what happened.
